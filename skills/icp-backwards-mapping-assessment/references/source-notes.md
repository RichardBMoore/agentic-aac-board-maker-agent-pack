# Source Notes

These notes summarise official sources checked on 2026-05-11. For policy-sensitive advice, exact compliance wording, or future use, verify the current official source before finalising.

## Queensland ICP And Assessment

Source: Queensland Department of Education, K-12 curriculum, assessment and reporting framework.

Useful anchors:

- Schools provide the Australian Curriculum to all students and provide an ICP when a student requires an adjustment to cognitive expectations in some or all learning areas or subjects.
- Assessment tasks and marking guides should target aspects of the relevant achievement standard.
- Adjustments to assessment should maintain curriculum and assessment intent and integrity.
- An ICP may support assessment and reporting against an achievement standard one or more years above or below enrolled year level, or adjusted learning focuses for a highly individualised curriculum plan.
- A student consistently performing at the A or E standard does not automatically require an ICP.
- Evidence for ICP decisions should demonstrate performance against relevant achievement standards and an appropriate level of challenge.
- The framework lists reasons that cannot be used in isolation for an ICP, including literacy or numeracy difficulty, SAE as an additional language or dialect, part-time educational program, behaviour, attendance, disengagement, or not yet having suitable AAC.

Official link: https://education.qld.gov.au/curriculums/Documents/k-12-curriculum-assessment-reporting-framework.pdf

## Australian Curriculum V9.0

Source: ACARA, About the curriculum content elements.

Useful anchors:

- Achievement standards describe the expected quality of learning students typically demonstrate by the end of a year or band.
- Content descriptions specify essential knowledge, understanding, and skills for teaching and learning.
- Content elaborations are optional examples and illustrations, not a mandatory complete set of content points.

Official link: https://www.australiancurriculum.edu.au/help/learning-areas/about-the-curriculum-content-elements

## NCCD Evidence

Source: Nationally Consistent Collection of Data on School Students with Disability, What evidence is the NCCD based upon?

Useful anchors:

- Schools retain evidence of provisions for students; they are not required to create new or additional evidence solely for NCCD.
- Evidence is contextual and may include assessed individual needs, adjustments provided, monitoring and review, and consultation or collaboration.
- Evidence examples include individualised or personalised learning planning, adjustment records, assessment-setting adjustments, adjusted materials, AAC supports, progress data, and parent/carer communication.

Official link: https://www.nccd.edu.au/wider-support-materials/what-evidence-nccd-based-upon

## Universal Design For Learning

Source: CAST, Universal Design for Learning.

Useful anchors:

- UDL anticipates learner variability and designs options into the environment.
- CAST organises UDL into engagement, representation, and action or expression.

Official link: https://www.cast.org/what-we-do/universal-design-for-learning/
