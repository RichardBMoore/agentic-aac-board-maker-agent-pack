## Rubric Structure

Use A-E only when it fits the school's reporting process and the target ICP level. For highly individualised plans, use progress descriptors approved by the school.

| Criterion | A / Exceeding | B | C / Expected target | D | E / Beginning |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Understanding or knowledge | Sophisticated or flexible evidence | Clear evidence | Expected evidence at target level | Partial evidence | Limited evidence |
| Skill or process | Accurate and independent where independence is part of the target | Mostly accurate | Accurate with expected support | Inconsistent | Emerging |
| Communication or representation | Clear, relevant, mode-appropriate | Mostly clear | Understandable using agreed mode | Limited clarity | Requires interpretation |
| Use of evidence or reasoning | Justified, connected, transferred | Explained | Identified or described as expected | Prompted or partial | Minimal |

Record support and independence separately unless they are explicitly part of the assessed construct.

## Final Quality Assurance Statement

```text
The student is learning in the same broad curriculum context as peers, with the curriculum judgement mapped to the approved ICP level or focus. The assessment is designed backwards from the achievement standard or learning focus, with valid evidence, accessible response pathways, documented support, and a clear basis for reporting and moderation.
```
