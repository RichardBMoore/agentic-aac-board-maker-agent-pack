# ICP / QCIA Checklist

## ICP assessment adaptation

Confirm:

- ICP level.
- Subject and year level.
- Original curriculum content and achievement standard link.
- Assessment technique/mode.
- Cognitive verbs to preserve.
- What evidence the student must produce.
- Language/literacy adjustment needed.
- Access method adjustment needed.
- Allowed scaffolds and supports.

Principles:

- Reduce access barriers, not curriculum intent.
- Prefer scaffolded choice/selection builders when writing load is the barrier.
- Keep assessment evidence visible and teacher-checkable.
- If modifying heavily, explicitly state what has changed and what curriculum intent remains.

## QCIA resource/evidence planning

Confirm:

- Student learning goal/s.
- Activity context.
- Observable evidence.
- Work sample/photo/teacher observation/reflection evidence.
- Student independence level.
- Communication/access supports used.
- How evidence maps to folio/reporting needs.

Good QCIA evidence should be:

- practical and authentic;
- easy for a teacher to verify;
- linked to goals;
- student-centred;
- not overloaded with generic text.
