# Teacher Intake Template

Use only when a missing answer materially changes the board. Accept partial answers, infer safe defaults, and record unresolved items in `systemFit` rather than blocking a useful draft.

```text
I can build this. Please share whatever you already know:

1. Purpose and context: what should the student communicate, participate in, or show—and in which lesson/routine?
2. Output: single-file HTML, printable board, Open AAC Studio JSON, or full resource pack?
3. Current communication system: what AAC/device/low-tech system is already used? Is this board a companion, temporary support, or planned addition?
4. Familiar vocabulary and layout: must-have words, stable word locations/motor patterns, known symbols/photos/text, and anything to avoid changing?
5. Access: touch/keyboard, eye gaze/dwell, switch, or partner-assisted? Known dwell/scan settings, reliable cancel/repair signal, and accidental-selection concerns?
6. Physical/visual setup: device/screen, browser, mount, seating/position, visual field, contrast, fatigue, and likely lighting?
7. Language and identity: preferred language(s), culturally meaningful terms/images, age/tone, and preferred speech voice/pronunciation?
8. Partner signals: reliable yes/no, stop, wait, finished, help, reject/“not that”, or other multimodal signals?
9. Symbols and privacy: preferred symbol set, representations already recognised, teacher-owned media, and any sensitive details that must stay local?
```

If unanswered, use anonymous Australian-English defaults, text fallback, touch/keyboard access, conservative density, explicit repair, and `systemFit.reviewStatus: "team-input-needed"`. Never imply that a generated board replaces the student's established AAC system or team review.
