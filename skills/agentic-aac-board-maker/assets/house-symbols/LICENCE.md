- Boards that embed them keep the attribution and must not be sold.
- Files are named `arasaac-<pictogram id>.png` (300 px).
- Each symbol's review status lives in `../../references/house-lexicon.json`: `proposed` means the agent chose it and the team has not reviewed it yet; `approved` means the team signed it off once for every board. Use `../../scripts/review_house_symbols.py` to review.
