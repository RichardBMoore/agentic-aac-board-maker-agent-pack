Choose a Class Activity

Look at, click, or tab to a choice. Hold gaze for 1.0 second to choose. Leaving the button cancels dwell.

Ready
Teacher notes, customisation, and QA

Purpose: An eye-gaze/dwell-safe AAC choice board that lets a student choose, request help, or repair the choice when selecting a class activity.

Model: Model the board by saying and selecting options yourself: “I choose Art”, “Help please”, “Different”. Wait after modelling and accept gaze, gesture, voice, partner interpretation, or keyboard selection.

Evidence: Teacher can note whether the student initiates a choice, confirms/rejects an activity, asks for help, or uses Different to repair the offered choices.

Customise: Replace the four activity labels/search terms with the real lesson choices. Keep Help and Different/Finished-style repair vocabulary stable where possible.

Access: Default dwell is 1000 ms. Targets are arranged 2 by 3 with large buttons, visible dwell progress, pointer-leave cancellation, and keyboard fallback.

Caveat: Draft classroom support only. Review with the teaching/SLP/OT team and test on the actual student device/access setup before relying on it.

Source of truth: gaze-choice-class-activity.ir.json. Privacy: anonymous, no student data. Symbol strategy: ARASAAC search terms with text fallback; HTML uses text/emoji placeholders only.
