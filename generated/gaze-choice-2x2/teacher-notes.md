# Teacher Notes - Choose a Class Activity

## Modelling

Model the board by saying and selecting options yourself: “I choose Art”, “Help please”, “Different”. Wait after modelling and accept gaze, gesture, voice, partner interpretation, or keyboard selection.

## Evidence

Teacher can note whether the student initiates a choice, confirms/rejects an activity, asks for help, or uses Different to repair the offered choices.

## Customisation

Replace the four activity labels/search terms with the real lesson choices. Keep Help and Different/Finished-style repair vocabulary stable where possible.

## Access

Default dwell is 1000 ms. Targets are arranged 2 by 3 with large buttons, visible dwell progress, pointer-leave cancellation, and keyboard fallback.

## Not a full AAC system

Draft classroom support only. Review with the teaching/SLP/OT team and test on the actual student device/access setup before relying on it.
