QCIA Community Access: Going to the Shops

A community access AAC board that supports practical shopping communication, safety/help language, and teacher evidence notes for QCIA-style participation.

Access: touch, keyboard, mouse, eye-gaze-dwell. Scan/order note: left-to-right, top-to-bottom.

Ready
Teacher notes, customisation, and QA

Modelling: Model real community phrases before the trip and during role play: greeting, asking where something is, paying, asking for help and saying stop/wait.

Evidence: QCIA evidence can note participation in a familiar community routine, functional communication attempts, help-seeking, choice-making and safety language.

Customisation: Replace shop/item vocabulary with the actual excursion, class enterprise or role-play context. Keep Help, Stop/wait and Finished stable.

Privacy: Use de-identified notes only. Do not record names, behaviour details or community incidents inside the generated files.

Safety: This supports communication during community access; it does not replace adult supervision, risk assessment or emergency processes.

Caveat: Draft classroom support only. Review with the teaching/SLP/OT team and test with the actual student, access method, device and school environment before relying on it.

Source of truth: qcia-community-shops.ir.json. Privacy: anonymous, no student data. Symbol strategy: ARASAAC search terms with text fallback; HTML uses text-only placeholders.
