Teacher notes

Model: Model real community phrases before the trip and during role play: greeting, asking where something is, paying, asking for help and saying stop/wait.

Evidence: QCIA evidence can note participation in a familiar community routine, functional communication attempts, help-seeking, choice-making and safety language.

Customise: Replace shop/item vocabulary with the actual excursion, class enterprise or role-play context. Keep Help, Stop/wait and Finished stable.
