Partner card: QCIA Community Access: Going to the Shops

1. Model these words this week

Touch the button on the board as you say the word in your own talk. Don’t ask the student to copy you.

  • Where is it? (Shop talk page, centre)
  • I want to buy this (Choosing page, top left)
  • Ready to pay — says “I'm ready to pay” (At the counter page, top left)
  • Thank you (At the counter page, top right)

2. Wait

After you model, comment or ask, wait at least 15 seconds. Count in your head and keep an expectant look.

3. Comment more than you question

  • Touch Where is it?: "I can't see the bread. Where is it?"
  • Touch Ready to pay while you wait at the counter for your own item.
  • Touch Thank you as you leave the counter.

Avoid test questions you already know the answer to.

4. If more support is needed (least to most)

  1. Model the word yourself on the board while you talk. Don't ask the student to copy you.
  2. Wait with an expectant look: count the wait time in your head.
  3. Comment on what is happening, then wait again.
  4. Ask an open question, then wait.
  5. Point near the part of the board (not the button), then wait.
  6. Never move the student's hand or body to the board (no hand-over-hand).

5. Every way counts

Respond to speech, sounds, looks, gestures and the board. Help is bottom-left on every page; Undo is bottom-middle wherever it appears; Stop is top-right wherever it appears; Speak is top-right wherever it appears; ABC is left-middle of the first page. Page buttons: ▶ forward is right-middle, ◀ back is left-middle. Stop speech appears at the right edge while the board is talking.

Practise in class role play first. In the shop, stand beside the student, not between them and the shop assistant. Stop, Wait, Yes and No keep the same cells on every shop page.

Board notes

Model: Model real community phrases before the trip and during role play: greeting, asking where something is, choosing, answering Yes or No, paying, and saying Stop or Wait.

Evidence: QCIA evidence can note participation in a familiar community routine, functional communication attempts, help-seeking, choice-making and safety language.

Customise: Replace shop and item vocabulary with the actual excursion, class enterprise or role-play context. Help, Stop, Wait, Yes and No keep the same cells on every shop page; keep them there when you change the topic words.
