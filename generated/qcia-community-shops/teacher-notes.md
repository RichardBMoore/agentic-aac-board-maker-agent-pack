# Teacher notes - QCIA Community Access: Going to the Shops

Fixture: `qcia-community-shops`

## Purpose

A community access AAC board that supports practical shopping communication, safety/help language, and teacher evidence notes for QCIA-style participation.

## Notes

### Modelling

Model real community phrases before the trip and during role play: greeting, asking where something is, paying, asking for help and saying stop/wait.

### Evidence

QCIA evidence can note participation in a familiar community routine, functional communication attempts, help-seeking, choice-making and safety language.

### Customisation

Replace shop/item vocabulary with the actual excursion, class enterprise or role-play context. Keep Help, Stop/wait and Finished stable.

### Privacy

Use de-identified notes only. Do not record names, behaviour details or community incidents inside the generated files.

### Safety

This supports communication during community access; it does not replace adult supervision, risk assessment or emergency processes.

## Privacy and caveat

Anonymous draft classroom support only. Review with the teaching/SLP/OT team and test with the actual student, access method, device and school environment before relying on it.
