# Teacher notes - Partner-Assisted Scanning Print Board

Fixture: `partner-assisted-print`

## Purpose

A printable partner-assisted scanning board with clear scan order, wait/confirm script, help, stop, different, finished and choice messages.

## How it works

- **Talk** (3x3): Yes, No, Stop, ABC, More time, Choices ▶, Help, Different, Finished.
- **Choices** (3x3): Choice A, Choice B, ◀ Talk, Core words ▶, Help.

The house standards tool added a **Core words** page (I, want, don't, like, go, more) that builds messages in the message bar, a **Word endings** page (-s, -ing, -ed, with Undo and Start again) and an **ABC keyboard** (QWERTY letter groups, then letters; Speak, Delete and Help on the keyboard page). Talking pages link forward with the right-middle button and back with the left-middle button; ABC sits left-middle on the first page.

## Partner card (summary)

- Model this week: Yes, No, More time, Different.
- Wait at least 15 seconds after you model, comment or ask.
- Comment more than you question, for example: Point to More time and say: "We have lots of time."
- If more support is needed, go from least to most help and never move the student's hand (no hand-over-hand).

Print `partner-card.html` for everyone who talks with the student.

## Notes

### Modelling

Model the scan process with another adult first: say each numbered option slowly, wait, observe the agreed signal, and confirm the selected message before acting.

### Customisation

Review vocabulary, symbols and positions with the student and team before use.

### Partner script

Partner says each option slowly in numbered order, waits, watches for the agreed signal, then confirms: I saw you choose __. Is that right?

### Scan order

Scan left to right, top to bottom: 1 Help, 2 Stop, 3 Different, 4 Finished, 5 Choice A, 6 Choice B, 7 Yes, 8 No, 9 More time.

### Wait confirm

Pause long enough for the student to respond. If unsure, repeat the scan or offer Yes/No confirmation without rushing.

### Print

Print in black and white if needed. Strong borders, large labels and numbered order are included for readability.

### Attribution

If adding symbols, record the symbol source and licence. Text-only printing is usable without symbols.

## Evidence

agreed signal; confirmed choice; repair request; refusal; more time request

Partner interpretation and scan repetitions are access context, not evidence of lower understanding.
