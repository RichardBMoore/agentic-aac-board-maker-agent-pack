# Teacher notes - Partner-Assisted Scanning Print Board

Fixture: `partner-assisted-print`

## Purpose

A printable partner-assisted scanning board with clear scan order, wait/confirm script, help, stop, different, finished and choice messages.

## Notes

### Partner Script

Partner says each option slowly in numbered order, waits, watches for the agreed signal, then confirms: I saw you choose __. Is that right?

### Scan Order

Scan left to right, top to bottom: 1 Help, 2 Stop, 3 Different, 4 Finished, 5 Choice A, 6 Choice B, 7 Yes, 8 No, 9 More time.

### Wait Confirm

Pause long enough for the student to respond. If unsure, repeat the scan or offer Yes/No confirmation without rushing.

### Print

Print in black and white if needed. Strong borders, large labels and numbered order are included for readability.

### Attribution

If adding symbols, record the symbol source and licence. Text-only printing is usable without symbols.

## Privacy and caveat

Anonymous draft classroom support only. Review with the teaching/SLP/OT team and test with the actual student, access method, device and school environment before relying on it.
