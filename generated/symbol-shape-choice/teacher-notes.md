# Teacher notes - Choose a shape

Fixture: `symbol-shape-choice`

## Purpose

Illustrative symbol-rendering fixture: choose a shape, request help or something different. Team review is needed for learner familiarity.

## How it works

- **Main** (2x3): Circle, Square, Triangle, Help, Different, Star.

No literacy pages: Early choice board smaller than 3x3; spelling and core words stay on the student's main system.

## Partner card (summary)

- Model this week: Circle, Square, Star, Different.
- Wait at least 15 seconds after you model, comment or ask.
- Comment more than you question, for example: Touch Star: "That star has five points."
- If more support is needed, go from least to most help and never move the student's hand (no hand-over-hand).

Print `partner-card.html` for everyone who talks with the student.

## Notes

### Modelling

Model choosing a shape, Help and Different. These original geometric symbols demonstrate offline image rendering; they are not a prescribed AAC symbol set.

### Customisation

Replace symbols with familiar representations after team review. Preserve chosen cell locations.

### Access

Default dwell is 1000 ms. Targets are arranged 2 by 3 with large buttons, visible dwell progress, pointer-leave cancellation, and keyboard fallback.

### Caveat

Draft classroom support only. Review with the teaching/SLP/OT team and test on the actual student device/access setup before relying on it.

## Evidence

Teacher can note whether the student initiates a choice, confirms/rejects an activity, asks for help, or uses Different to repair the offered choices.

Dwell timing and accidental cancellation reflect access tuning, not preference certainty.

## Symbols

6 of 6 different word and message buttons show a symbol; 2 of these were proposed by the agent and await team review. Letters and page buttons are text by design. Text-only words: none.

Proposed symbols to review (ARASAAC id): Help (#32648), Different (#4628). Review house words once with `review_house_symbols.py` and topic words with `fetch_arasaac_symbols.py --review-out`.

## Privacy and caveat

Anonymous draft classroom support only. Review with the teaching/SLP/OT team and test with the actual student, access method, device and school environment before relying on it.
