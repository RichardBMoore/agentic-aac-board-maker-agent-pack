Partner card: Year 7 Hero Speech Sentence Builder

1. Model these words this week

Touch the button on the board as you say the word in your own talk. Don’t ask the student to copy you.

  • My hero is… — says “My hero is” (My hero page, top left)
  • I think they are (My hero page, centre)
  • because (Reasons page, top left)
  • brave (My hero page, bottom right)

2. Wait

After you model, comment or ask, wait at least 15 seconds. Count in your head and keep an expectant look.

3. Comment more than you question

  • Build "I think they are brave because they help others." while you talk about the class text.
  • Model your own hero with ABC: "My hero is" + spell "nan" + "because they help others".

Avoid test questions you already know the answer to.

4. If more support is needed (least to most)

  1. Model the word yourself on the board while you talk. Don't ask the student to copy you.
  2. Wait with an expectant look: count the wait time in your head.
  3. Comment on what is happening, then wait again.
  4. Ask an open question, then wait.
  5. Point near the part of the board (not the button), then wait.
  6. Never move the student's hand or body to the board (no hand-over-hand).

5. Every way counts

Respond to speech, sounds, looks, gestures and the board. Help is bottom-left on every page; Undo is bottom-middle wherever it appears; Speak is top-right on every page; ABC is left-middle of the first page. Page buttons: ▶ forward is right-middle, ◀ back is left-middle. Stop speech appears at the right edge while the board is talking.

Accept partial sentences as drafting. Opinion words are on My hero; reasons are on Reasons, one page turn away. The student can spell their own hero with ABC.
