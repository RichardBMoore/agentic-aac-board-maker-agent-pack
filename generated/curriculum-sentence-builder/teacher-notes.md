# Teacher notes - Year 7 Hero Speech Sentence Builder

Fixture: `curriculum-sentence-builder`

## Purpose

A two-page sentence-builder board for a Year 7 English hero speech. The student adds words to a sentence bar, speaks the whole sentence, and uses opinion, reason, evidence, and repair language to rehearse a short spoken text.

## How it works

- **My hero** (3x3): My hero is…, a firefighter, Speak, ABC, I think they are, Reasons ▶, Help, Undo, brave.
- **Reasons** (3x3): because, they help others, Speak, ◀ My hero, For example…, Core words ▶, Help, Undo, they rescue people.

The house standards tool added a **Core words** page (I, want, don't, like, go, more) that builds messages in the message bar, a **Word endings** page (-s, -ing, -ed, with Undo and Start again) and an **ABC keyboard** (QWERTY letter groups, then letters; Speak, Delete and Help on the keyboard page). Talking pages link forward with the right-middle button and back with the left-middle button; ABC sits left-middle on the first page.

## Partner card (summary)

- Model this week: My hero is…, I think they are, because, brave.
- Wait at least 15 seconds after you model, comment or ask.
- Comment more than you question, for example: Build "I think they are brave because they help others." while you talk about the class text.
- If more support is needed, go from least to most help and never move the student's hand (no hand-over-hand).

Print `partner-card.html` for everyone who talks with the student.

## Notes

### Modelling

Model a complete chain: My hero is, a firefighter, I think they are, brave, then Reasons, because, they help others, then Speak. Accept partial sentences as valid drafting or rehearsal.

### Customisation

The firefighter is demonstration content, not evidence from a class text. Replace the hero words with the class text, film or local hero; the student can spell any hero's name with the ABC keyboard on the first page.

### Curriculum

Supports Year 7 English speaking/listening by giving access to opinion, reasoning and evidence language for a short hero speech, and by letting the student assemble and rehearse a whole sentence rather than single words.

### Repair

Undo is bottom-middle on both content pages; Start again is on the Word endings page; Help is bottom-left everywhere.

### Use

Use for planning, rehearsal or supported oral presentation. The student builds a sentence on one page, crosses to the describing-words page, then speaks the whole sentence. Do not treat the board as the student's full expressive system.

## Evidence

sentence built in the message bar; opinion/because/example selection; speak-sentence use; repair (undo, start again) or help request

Prompting, navigation support, or access method should contextualise the speech evidence, not replace judgement about English content.

## Symbols
