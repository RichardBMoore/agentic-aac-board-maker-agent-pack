Partner card: Secondary Needs and Communication Repair Board

1. Model these words this week

Touch the button on the board as you say the word in your own talk. Don’t ask the student to copy you.

  • Break — says “I need a break” (I need page, top left)
  • Say it another way — says “Please say it another way” (Sort it out page, top left)
  • I disagree (Sort it out page, top right)
  • I need privacy (I need page, centre)

2. Wait

After you model, comment or ask, wait at least 15 seconds. Count in your head and keep an expectant look.

3. Comment more than you question

  • Touch Break: "I need a break after that too."
  • Touch Say it another way when you rephrase your own instructions.
  • Touch I disagree during a class discussion and give your reason.

Avoid test questions you already know the answer to.

4. If more support is needed (least to most)

  1. Model the word yourself on the board while you talk. Don't ask the student to copy you.
  2. Wait with an expectant look: count the wait time in your head.
  3. Comment on what is happening, then wait again.
  4. Ask an open question, then wait.
  5. Point near the part of the board (not the button), then wait.
  6. Never move the student's hand or body to the board (no hand-over-hand).

5. Every way counts

Respond to speech, sounds, looks, gestures and the board. Help is bottom-left on every page; Different is bottom-middle wherever it appears; Undo is bottom-middle wherever it appears; Finished is bottom-right wherever it appears; Speak is top-right wherever it appears; ABC is left-middle of the first page. Page buttons: ▶ forward is right-middle, ◀ back is left-middle. Stop speech appears at the right edge while the board is talking.

Every message on this board is honoured, including Finished and I disagree. Respond to it; do not treat it as behaviour.
