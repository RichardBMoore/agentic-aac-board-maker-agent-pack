# Teacher notes - Secondary Needs and Communication Repair Board

Fixture: `needs-repair-board`

## Purpose

An age-respectful two-page needs and communication repair board that lets a secondary student request support, privacy, clarification, a break, different choices or communication repair without behaviour-control framing. Help stays on every page and no page exceeds nine targets.

## How it works

- **I need** (3x3): Break, Wait, I feel unwell, ABC, I need privacy, Sort it out ▶, Help, Too loud, Finished.
- **Sort it out** (3x3): Say it another way, Not that, I disagree, ◀ I need, Can I choose?, Core words ▶, Help, Different.

The house standards tool added a **Core words** page (I, want, don't, like, go, more) that builds messages in the message bar, a **Word endings** page (-s, -ing, -ed, with Undo and Start again) and an **ABC keyboard** (QWERTY letter groups, then letters; Speak, Delete and Help on the keyboard page). Talking pages link forward with the right-middle button and back with the left-middle button; ABC sits left-middle on the first page.

## Partner card (summary)

- Model this week: Break, Say it another way, I disagree, I need privacy.
- Wait at least 15 seconds after you model, comment or ask.
- Comment more than you question, for example: Touch Break: "I need a break after that too."
- If more support is needed, go from least to most help and never move the student's hand (no hand-over-hand).

Print `partner-card.html` for everyone who talks with the student.

## Notes

### Modelling

Model the board neutrally during calm moments, including how to move between the two pages. Treat selections as communication, not behaviour compliance.

### Customisation

Review vocabulary, symbols and positions with the student and team before use.

### Age respectful

Language is plain and secondary-appropriate. Avoid childish praise, token language or public commentary about private needs.

### Privacy

Do not log sensitive health, behaviour or family details in the file. Use separate approved school processes for confidential notes.

### Repair

Honour repair messages such as Not that, Different, Say it another way and Wait before repeating demands.

### Navigation

Page 1 is urgent needs and regulation; page 2 is repair and self-advocacy. Help please is on both pages so support is always one selection away.

### Use

Use as a support for needs and communication repair, not as a behaviour-control board or replacement AAC system.

## Evidence

support request; privacy request; repair message; refusal; question or disagreement

Needs/repair selections are communication and wellbeing context, not behaviour scoring.
