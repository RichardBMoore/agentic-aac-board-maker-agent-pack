Partner card: Morning Routine Expressive Visual Schedule

1. Model these words this week

Touch the button on the board as you say the word in your own talk. Don’t ask the student to copy you.

  • What's next? (Morning routine page, bottom middle)
  • Wait — says “Please wait” (Talk about it page, top left)
  • I like it (Talk about it page, top right)
  • Finished — says “I'm finished” (Morning routine page, bottom right)

2. Wait

After you model, comment or ask, wait at least 15 seconds. Count in your head and keep an expectant look.

3. Comment more than you question

  • Touch What's next? as you check the schedule together.
  • Touch Finished when a step ends: the schedule moves Now to the next step.
  • Touch I like it about a step you enjoy.

Avoid test questions you already know the answer to.

4. If more support is needed (least to most)

  1. Model the word yourself on the board while you talk. Don't ask the student to copy you.
  2. Wait with an expectant look: count the wait time in your head.
  3. Comment on what is happening, then wait again.
  4. Ask an open question, then wait.
  5. Point near the part of the board (not the button), then wait.
  6. Never move the student's hand or body to the board (no hand-over-hand).

5. Every way counts

Respond to speech, sounds, looks, gestures and the board. Help is bottom-left on every page; Finished is bottom-right wherever it appears; Speak is top-right wherever it appears; ABC is left-middle of the first page. Page buttons: ▶ forward is right-middle, ◀ back is left-middle. Stop speech appears at the right edge while the board is talking.

Finished marks the current step done. Adults can also mark or undo steps in the teacher panel.

Board notes

Model: Point to schedule steps as they happen and model expressive messages, especially Wait, Help please, Change and Finished.

Evidence: Note whether the student anticipates next steps, requests help/wait/change, or comments on readiness.

Customise: Replace routine steps with the actual class morning sequence and photos if available and approved.
