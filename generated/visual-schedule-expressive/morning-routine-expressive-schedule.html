Morning Routine Expressive Visual Schedule

A visual schedule that shows morning routine order while preserving expressive options for waiting, help, change, finished and student comments.

Access: touch, keyboard, mouse, eye-gaze-dwell. Scan/order note: schedule order, then expressive supports.

Ready
Teacher notes, customisation, and QA

Modelling: Point to schedule steps as they happen and model expressive messages, especially Wait, Help please, Change and Finished.

Schedule Order: The first row and early second-row buttons are the routine sequence; the remaining buttons are expressive supports, not compliance controls.

Customisation: Replace routine steps with the actual class morning sequence and photos if available and approved.

Not Full Aac Claim: This is a visual schedule with communication supports. It is not a replacement for the student's full AAC system.

Evidence: Note whether the student anticipates next steps, requests help/wait/change, or comments on readiness.

Caveat: Draft classroom support only. Review with the teaching/SLP/OT team and test with the actual student, access method, device and school environment before relying on it.
